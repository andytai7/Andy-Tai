Our Grand Challenges submission, *Measuring Severity* (with Nico Kallenberg, Jens Kleesiek, and Michael Kamp), was accepted to [AXIOM 2026: Foundations of Efficient Deep Learning](https://axiom-neurips2026.github.io/), the NeurIPS 2026 workshop on predictive principles for efficient AI. The paper argues that expert-labeled severity of wrong answers is a missing evaluation primitive, most urgent when selecting efficient models for clinical prediction. [Paper on OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=AFWMSe0WOT).
