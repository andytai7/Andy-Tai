Capstone projects supervised or evaluated include:

- **External Demand Signals for Handheld Ultrasound** — Clarius Mobile Health: country-by-state-by-channel panel (58 months), Negative Binomial GLM, Bayesian mixed GLM, Bayesian Structural Time Series. *Role: presentation evaluation.*
- **CPOE Impact Evaluation on Pediatric Laboratory Services** — BC Children's Hospital, Dept. of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine (sponsor: Dr. Vilte Barakauskas): CPOE evaluation around the 2022 CST Cerner go-live, SQL linkage of Sunquest LIS and Cerner EHR extracts within the PHSA PANDA secure enclave. *Role: supervision.*
- **Deep Learning for Soil Geochemistry Prediction** — VRIFY Technology: ~130 GeoTIFF covariates, Recursive Feature Elimination, spatially blocked StratifiedGroupKFold, CNN/U-Net/DOFA foundation models, SHAP attribution, MLflow. *Role: presentation evaluation.*
- **MedDRA Autocoder** — Vaccine Evaluation Center, BCCHR: biomedical sentence embeddings (SapBERT, BioLORD, BiomedBERT) with lexical re-weighting; Streamlit reviewer-adjudication app; Top-3 retrieval accuracy 0.887. *Role: supervision.*
- **CDL Venture Dashboard** — Creative Destruction Lab: Cox PH survival analysis, XGBoost/HistGB graduation prediction, KNN similarity, GPT-4 feature extraction, dockerized Plotly Dash app. *Role: supervision.*
- **Automated Phishing Detection** — UBC Cybersecurity: containerized email-classification web service with monitoring dashboard. *Role: supervision.*
- **Semi-Supervised Vessel Trajectories** — Nautical Crime Investigation Services: AIS data (~20 GB), HMM segmentation, LSTM sequence labeling; led to a published journal article (EAAI 2026). *Role: supervision.*
