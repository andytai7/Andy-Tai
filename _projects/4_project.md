Published R package (CRAN) for automated auditing of ggplot2 visualizations for accessibility, readability, and misleading practices: color vision deficiency simulation, WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios, detection of truncated axes, small text, missing labels, inadequate point sizes, and color-only encoding, with automated fix suggestions.
