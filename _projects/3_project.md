My current research program at the **Institute for Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (IKIM)**, University Hospital Essen, develops federated and privacy-preserving learning methods that let clinical models learn from distributed hospital data **without centralizing patient records**, with explicit accounting of communication cost, privacy, and reliability.

## FLIP-IT

**Federated chronic kidney disease risk prediction across general practice clinics**, funded by NEXT.IN.NRW and built on the Flower framework. Collaboration with Prof. Jens Kleesiek (IKIM), Prof. Michael Kamp (Lamarr Institute / TU Dortmund), and Dr. Moon Kim, including collaboration with the Kamp Lab at TU Dortmund.

## Brain-inspired federated continual learning

Privacy-preserving federated continual learning algorithms, **federated synaptic consolidation**, **federated hippocampal replay**, and **predictive-coding FL**, that allow clinical models to learn continuously from distributed hospital data without catastrophic forgetting. Deliverables include an open-source federated continual learning benchmark toolkit.

## Activation and rank-code communication protocols

A methods project [work in progress; no publication attached] on federated protocols in which clients communicate **activation or rank codes**, which units fire, and in what order, rather than model parameters, with pre-registered control arms and explicit communication-bitrate accounting.
